02 — About

About the studio

We are a small independent creative studio building identities, websites and digital experiences for ambitious brands.

Founded in Paris in 2014, NOCTIS works with founders, cultural institutions and global brands who believe the details are the product. We keep the team small on purpose: every project is led by the people who design and build it.

Our clients come to us for a website and stay for everything around it — identity systems, product interfaces, launch films and installations. Different media, one conviction: people remember how things make them feel.

03 — Services

What we do

Six disciplines, one team. Most projects combine three or four of them — we assemble the right people from day one.

  • Strategy, naming and visual systems designed to flex across every screen, space and surface — built to move, not just to sit on a page.

    • Strategy
    • Naming
    • Logotype
    • Guidelines

  • Editorial, art-directed websites where hierarchy, rhythm and motion are designed together, from the first sketch to the last pixel.

    • Art direction
    • UX architecture
    • Interface
    • Design system

  • Next.js, WebGL and GSAP engineered in-house. We prototype in code from day one, so ideas survive all the way to production.

    • Front-end
    • WebGL
    • Headless CMS
    • Performance

  • Conversational products, generative tools and agent interfaces that stay useful, legible and honest about what they know.

    • AI product design
    • Prototyping
    • Conversation design
    • Evaluation

  • Research, UX and design systems for digital products people open every day — and would miss if they disappeared.

    • Research
    • UX
    • UI
    • Design ops

  • Real-time 3D, motion languages and interactive installations that give brands a physical presence on the screen.

    • Motion language
    • Real-time 3D
    • Installations
    • Launch films
